The shop

Built by collectors, for collectors

Darksteel Forge started the way most card shops do: a binder, a folding table and too many trades that should have gone the other way. It grew into a stocked inventory of Magic and Pokemon singles, sealed product and the chase cards that never seem to be in stock anywhere else.

Every card that comes through the door is inspected under light, sleeved and stored in a climate-controlled room. Condition is graded honestly, prices track the market daily, and if we do not have the card you want we will source it. When the storefront opens it will have play space, weekly events and a buylist counter. Until then, everything here ships.

Questions

Good to know

How do you grade condition?

Near Mint, Lightly Played, Moderately Played and Heavily Played, following TCGplayer's standard. Every card is checked front and back under light for whitening, scratches, clouding and bends. If we are between two grades we call it the lower one.

Where do your prices come from?

Magic prices follow Scryfall's market data and Pokemon prices follow TCGplayer market, refreshed daily. Played conditions are priced as a percentage of Near Mint. A quote you receive is locked for 48 hours.

Do you buy cards?

Yes. Send a list or photos and you will get a written offer in cash or store credit (credit pays more). Bulk, collections and sealed are all welcome.

Are the cards authentic?

Every high-value card is checked with a light test, a loupe and, for Magic, the rosette pattern. We do not sell proxies, reprints passed as originals, or altered cards without disclosure.
