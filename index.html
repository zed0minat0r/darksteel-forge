Darksteel Forge Gaming is…

Darksteel Forge Gaming is downtown Phoenixville's community game store. A safe, welcoming space to gather around the games you love. We specialize in trading card games like Magic: The Gathering, Pokemon, One Piece, and Riftbound, and we carry RPGs, board games, supplies, and plenty of table space to play. Come find your people.

Parking

  • Free on Bridge Street. Two-hour street parking on the 100, 200 and 300 blocks, and on the side streets around them.
  • Borough lots, $2 an hour. Main & Bridge, Taylor Alley, Mill St and Prospect St. Pay at the kiosk or with the Passport Parking app.
  • Evenings and weekends. The Borough Hall lot is $1 an hour weeknights after 5pm and all weekend.

Lot rules and rates: phoenixville.org/parking
